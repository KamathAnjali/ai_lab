No, the most probable predictions are not always the same as the words that a person would personally expect. The model bases its predictions only on the transition probabilities learned from the training data, while humans use broader knowledge, context, meaning, grammar, and real-world experience.

This shows that a probability model learns statistical patterns from data, whereas human linguistic expectations involve more than just word frequencies and immediate word-to-word relationships. A simple first-order model has limited context, so it may make predictions that are statistically likely but not necessarily the most natural or meaningful to a human.
