The network makes a first-order Markov assumption. This means that each word depends only on the immediately preceding word and is conditionally independent of all earlier words given the previous word.

In probability notation:

P(X_t | X_1, X_2, ..., X_{t-1}) = P(X_t | X_{t-1})

For example:

P(X_3 | X_1, X_2) = P(X_3 | X_2)

Thus, once X_(t-1) is known, the earlier words do not provide additional information about X_t.
